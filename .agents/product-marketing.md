# Product marketing context — Yeskoko / Edis Mart

**Document version:** 1.0  
**Changelog:** 2026-09-12 — Initial context from live site audit and rebuild brief.

## Product

Yeskoko is a Ghanaian dried-fruit snack line (coconut flakes, mango chips, pineapple chips) produced by Edis Mart in Accra. V1 storefront sells snacks only.

## Audience

- Health-conscious retail buyers in Accra / Ghana
- Parents and professionals wanting preservative-free snacks
- Retailers, hotels, and kitchens needing wholesale sizes

## Positioning

Premium natural snacks that still taste like real fruit. Made in Ghana. Honest sizes from 50g to 1kg. Pay how Ghanaians already pay (MoMo, card, COD).

## Differentiation

- Local production and Accra delivery/pickup
- Clear retail vs wholesale path
- Packaging-forward brand (Yeskoko) with credible company story (Edis Mart)
- No preservatives claim aligned with founder biochemistry credibility

## Offer

- Retail variable sizes at GHS prices
- Wholesale via WhatsApp / inquiry (not public undercutting twins)
- Pineapple chips as batch request until priced

## Primary conversion

Add to cart → Paystack or COD. Secondary: WhatsApp order. Tertiary: wholesale message.

## Brand voice

Proud, warm, specific, crunchy. No startup filler.

## Proof available today

- Real packaging photography
- Founder bios
- Phone + hours + email
- Existing Paystack merchant relationship

## Proof gaps

- Reviews, ratings, press, exact farm sourcing stories (add when client provides)

## Competitors (category)

Generic supermarket dried fruit, imported snacks, informal market coconut. Win on packaging, trust, and e-commerce ease.
