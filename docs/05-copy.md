## FAQ

**Headline:** Questions, answered  
**Intro:** Payment, delivery, sizes, and how we make the snacks — short answers for Accra shoppers and wholesale buyers.

**Q/A:**
1. How do I pay? — Card or Mobile Money through Paystack, cash on delivery in Accra, or WhatsApp order confirmation.
2. Do you deliver in Accra? — Yes. A flat Accra delivery fee is added at checkout. Pickup can be arranged when you leave a note.
3. What sizes do you sell? — Retail sizes from 50g to 1kg depending on the product. Wholesale packs are available on request.
4. Are there preservatives? — Yeskoko snacks are made without preservatives. Ingredients stay close to the fruit.
5. How do wholesale orders work? — Message us on WhatsApp with sizes and volume. We quote and confirm delivery from Accra.
6. Is pineapple chips available? — Packaging exists. Use Request a batch on the pineapple product page and we will quote.
7. How should I store Yeskoko? — Keep pouches sealed in a cool, dry place. Reseal after opening so the crunch stays.
8. How long do the snacks keep? — Dried fruit keeps well when sealed. Check the pack date and finish open pouches while the texture is at its best.
9. Can I pick up instead of delivery? — Yes. Leave a pickup note at checkout or confirm on WhatsApp — we arrange from Accra.
